    50,000 NOVA to share today

    No server farms. No graphics cards. No presale and no coins set aside for the creator. Just a public contract that releases 50,000 NOVA every day and shares them among everyone who shows up, halving every 210 days, until 21 million.

    Rules nobody can change

    They are written into the contract. Not even its creator can touch them, and anyone can read the code on the block explorer.

    • 21 million. Not one more.

      The contract can never create more than 21,000,000 NOVA. The daily reward keeps halving, so the total gets closer and closer to that limit without ever passing it.

    • 50,000 NOVA a day, shared

      Every day the contract releases a fixed amount, split among every wallet that mined that day according to its mining power. No race: mining at 9:00 or at 23:00 gives the same share.

    • Holders mine more

      Your mining power goes from 1x with no NOVA up to 10x, growing with the NOVA you mined and still hold. Newcomers start small; coins sent to you by others don’t count.

    • Halving every 210 days

      The daily reward halves every 210 days, just like Bitcoin’s reward halves every 210,000 blocks. Early miners get more, and NOVA gets scarcer over time.

    • Nobody can take it all

      At most one daily reward can be created per day, whatever happens. Not even someone with a thousand wallets can empty the supply.

    • The creator starts at zero

      No reserved coins. Whoever launched Nova has to mine just like everyone else.

    FAQ

    Does mining cost money?

    The coin is free, but every transaction on Base pays a small network fee (gas) in ETH. It is usually a few cents.

    Is this mining like Bitcoin?

    Closer than you’d think. Like Bitcoin, a fixed reward is released on a schedule, it halves over time (every 210 days here, every 210,000 blocks there) and the total stops at 21 million. The difference: in Bitcoin, computers compete to solve calculations; here, the day’s reward is split among everyone who pressed Mine, weighted by how much NOVA they hold.

    Why do I get my coins the next day?

    Your share depends on how many people mine that day, and that is only known when the day closes at 00:00 UTC. It is paid automatically the next time you mine, or you can claim it any time with the “Claim” button.

    Can someone take all the coins?

    No. At most 50,000 NOVA can be created per day, whatever happens. New wallets start at 1x mining power, so creating many empty wallets gets little, and each one pays gas every day. Nobody can empty the supply or stop others from getting their share.

    What is mining power?

    It decides how big your slice of the day is. A wallet with no NOVA mines at 1x. Power grows with the NOVA you mined yourself and still hold, up to 10x when you hold two days’ worth of the daily reward (100,000 NOVA in the first 210 days). It grows fastest at the start: 1,000 NOVA gives 1.9x, 10,000 gives 3.8x and 25,000 gives 5.5x. NOVA sent to you by someone else doesn’t count, and if you send yours away, your power goes down.

    Why Base?

    Base is a layer-2 network built on Ethereum. It inherits Ethereum’s security, but fees are much lower, so one transaction a day makes sense.

    Is it worth anything? Can I buy it?

    No. It is an educational project: it is not sold and is not listed on any exchange. It exists to learn how tokens and smart contracts work.

    I can’t see my coins in MetaMask

    Click “Add NOVA to MetaMask” in the mining panel. The coin will show up in your wallet with its logo.
